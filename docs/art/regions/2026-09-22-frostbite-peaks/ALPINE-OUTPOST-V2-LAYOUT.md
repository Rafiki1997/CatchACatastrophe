# Alpine Outpost V2 — measured layout and build record

Frostbite Peaks, rebuilt for the 280 x 200 chain cell to
`frostbite-peaks-v2-02-alpine-outpost-refined.png`. Implementation:
`src/server/Map/FrostbitePeaks.luau` with `FrostbiteAlpineAssetSpec.luau` and
`FrostbiteAlpineProps.luau`. Asset contract: `ALPINE-OUTPOST-V2-ASSET-REQUEST.md`.
Every placement: `ALPINE-OUTPOST-V2-SITES.csv` (generated by the verifier).

**Status, plainly.** The layout, functional geometry, placement sites and swap
scaffolding are built and pass 8,694 headless checks. Astra's 28-mesh kit is
delivered as Blender/FBX and matches the contract, but it is **not yet uploaded
to Roblox or captured as `FrostbiteAlpineTemplates`**, so the game still shows
the temporary stand-ins. **Nothing here has been seen in Roblox Studio.** Every
image below is a Blender render of the harness dump, not a Studio capture.

## 1. How the concept was measured

V2 is an image-generation edit of the V1 option-02 render, and it is not a
consistent camera:

- The V1 camera (pitch 45, 200 mm, distance 1950) lands exactly on V1's walls.
  On V2 it misses by 41 px RMS: the generator redrew the projection.
- A free pinhole fitted to V2's 18 engine features (gate bases, gate lanterns,
  cell corners, divider piers) still leaves **11.75 px RMS**; letting the pixel
  aspect float only reaches 9.9. The systematic residual: **V2 draws the gate at
  its true 44 studs but the rest of the cell about 10% narrower** (its walls sit
  at +-127 gate-scale studs against the real +-138.5 inner face; its divider
  piers at 0.93-0.95 of their real positions), and it draws heights about 20%
  shorter than its own ground plane implies.
- V2's side-wall lanterns are not at the real pier spacing (their image gaps
  are 122 / 153 / 164 / 163 px where a camera would grow them smoothly), so they
  were not used as anchors.

Method, following Cinder Quarry and Splashwater Bay:

1. **Ground homography from the four gate-pier bases**, a known 44 x 200
   rectangle. Every measurement script is in `src/v2-measure/` (`homog.py`,
   `measure.py`, `annot.py`); the pixel list is in section 10.
2. **Piecewise x map** anchored on engine features V2 does show: gate piers
   (22 -> 22), inner divider piers (57.7 -> 61.33), outer divider piers
   (94.3 -> 100.67), wall face (126.5 -> 138.5). Side-band features therefore
   keep their distance from the walls and piers; the open meadow absorbs the
   difference, which is where the concept wants width.
3. **Heights from the gate piers' 15-stud teal panels** (64 px at the south gate,
   60 px at the north), multiplied by 1.12 to split V2's disagreement between
   that ruler and its ground plane. Heights are estimates, +-15%.
4. **Cross-check**: every landmark was re-read through the fitted comparison
   camera; positions agree with the homography to 1-2 studs.
5. **Projection back onto the concept** of every footprint at its real height:
   `v2-measured-layout-on-concept.png`. Plan: `v2-layout-plan.png`.

## 2. Axes — read from the code, not the camera

`RegionScenery` builds `frame = CFrame.lookAt(centre, (0, centre.Y, 0))`. The
Frost centre is world (0, 0, -915), so LookVector is world +Z and:

| Space | +X | +Y | +Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Region-local (all numbers here, the Luau, the CSV) | world **west**, concept image **left**, the lodge's side | up | world **north**, the Thunderworks gate |
| Roblox world | east | up | south (the hub) |
| Blender mockup scene (`src/*.py`) | east (= -local x) | north (= local z) | up |
| Asset-local (Astra) | the viewer's **left** when facing the asset's front | up | back; the front faces **-Z** |

Local -Z is the Cinder threshold (entry, south gate at z -100); local +Z the
Thunderworks gate (z +100). The old circular module's bearings are gone: the
lodge is at +X, the bridge at -X, exactly as the concept frames them. Absolute
y: the region floor's top face is 0.30.

## 4. Walking connections (verified headless by flood fill)

Step-up limit 0.75, headroom 4.5, drops allowed; every target is reached on
foot from the south gate apron:

- South gate -> straight 24-stud packed-snow route -> north gate (nothing above
  the floor in |x| < 12).
- Meadow -> terrace stair -> terrace deck (5.80) -> porch step (6.30) -> porch (6.80).
  The door is closed; the lodge has no interior, by design.
- Meadow -> shelf B stair -> shelf B (8.80) -> bridge (dips to 7.60) ->
  landing A (8.80) -> shelf C (8.80, signpost and rope rail). A/C is an overlook;
  a player leaves it the way they came or drops the 8.5 studs to the snow.
- Containment: invisible proxies over the side walls behind the terrace and
  behind shelf B and the gorge rock, where a walk level comes within a jump of
  the coping. RegionAccessService still enforces the whole cell volume.

## 9. Differences that remain, and why

- **Cinder's quarry in front.** From this camera Cinder Quarry's 42-stud mine
  and 36-stud terrace stand in front of Frost's south-west and south-east
  corners; V2 paints that strip as low rocks and cacti. Cinder is preserved as
  built. From inside Frost it is behind the south wall.
- **The camera itself.** No pinhole reproduces V2 better than 11.75 px RMS, so
  gates render about 10% narrower and all heights about 16% taller in pixels
  than V2 draws them. Proportions follow V2's piers, not its pixels.
- **Shelf and gorge art.** Astra's delivered shelves read from above as flat
  white slabs with thin dark bands, and the gorge rock as a column of snow
  balls; V2 draws layered, irregular slate under rounded snow. Up close they
  read better. Review note in the asset request.
- **Firs** are delivered at 15 x 26 (1.7:1); V2's are about 2.6:1. Heights are
  kept at V2's, so canopies run wider. A slender re-export is a recommended
  follow-up.
- **Lighting, smoke, snow sparkle, bloom** are Roblox's and can only be judged in
  Studio. The creatures are the game's own spawns, not scenery.

## 10. Reproduce

```
lune run tools/verify_frostbite_alpine.luau            # 8,694 checks, writes build/frostbite-alpine-*.{json,csv}
lune run tools/world_harness.luau map.json             # whole-map dump
blender -b --factory-startup -P tools/render_region_blender.py -- \
  --dump map.json --camera refcam.json --out build.png --zmin=-103.5 --zmax 112 \
  --exposure 0.15 --open-gate-z=-815 \
  --kit-blend assets/frostbite-peaks/alpine-v2/AlpineOutpost.blend \
  --sites build/frostbite-alpine-sites.csv            # drop the two --kit args for stand-ins
```

`refcam.json` (scene axes: X east, Y north, Z up, origin the region centre):
C = (-7.1, -947.9, 941.0); fitted yaw 0.46, pitch 45.30, roll 0.30 degrees;
fpx 6155 at 1536 x 1024 (144.3 mm on a 36 mm sensor). Pass negative argparse
values as `--x=-1`.

V2 feature pixels used for the fits: gate-pier bases (652, 832) (884, 832)
(662, 163) (872, 163); gate lanterns (652, 703) (884, 703) (662, 60) (872, 62);
inner corners at the wall top (101, 762) (1430, 762); north corner lanterns
(152, 113) (1378, 112); south divider piers (276, 812) (462, 812) (1070, 812)
(1270, 812); north inner pier lanterns (490, 98) (1027, 98).
