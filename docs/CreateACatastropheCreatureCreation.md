I am creating a Roblox game called **“Catch a Catastrophe”** where players collect unique pets from different catastrophe-themed areas.

I am currently designing a **COSMIC** area, and I want you to create **6 completely unique pet concepts** specifically for this area.

## 🚨 MOST IMPORTANT DESIGN RULE

I do **NOT** want generic animals with a galaxy texture slapped onto them.

The pets must be **fundamentally designed around actual cosmic objects, phenomena, or concepts**.

The cosmic inspiration should influence the pet's:

* Overall shape
* Silhouette
* Body structure
* Anatomy
* Features
* Materials
* Movement
* Personality
* Animations
* Visual effects

The pet should look like it was **born from or inspired by something in space**, rather than being a normal animal wearing a space-themed skin.

### ❌ DO NOT DO THIS

* Normal cat + purple galaxy texture
* Normal dog + stars on its body
* Normal dragon + blue space texture
* Normal bunny + planets floating around it
* Any basic animal with neon colors and stars added
* Simple recolors of the same animal

These would feel cheap and generic.

### ✅ DO THIS INSTEAD

The actual **form and concept of the pet** should come from something cosmic.

For example:

* A creature formed around a black hole
* A living supernova
* A creature made from asteroid fragments
* A living constellation
* A neutron-star-based creature
* A comet creature with a physical plasma tail
* A creature based on a planetary ring system
* A wormhole creature
* A dark-matter-inspired entity
* A miniature galaxy that has formed into a living creature

These are examples of the **type of creativity I want**, but do NOT simply use these exact examples. Come up with your own original ideas.

## 🧠 THE FLAT-COLOR TEST

For every pet, imagine removing **all textures, particles, glow, and special effects**.

If the pet was just a simple flat-colored 3D model, I should STILL be able to understand that its design is inspired by something cosmic.

If the cosmic identity disappears when the texture is removed, **the concept is not good enough and should be redesigned.**

The cosmic theme must come from the **actual 3D design**, not surface decoration.

* Cute but visually interesting
* Stylized Roblox aesthetic
* Strong silhouettes
* Easy to recognize at a glance
* Suitable for a Roblox pet-collecting game

Avoid making them overly realistic.

They should feel like **stylized collectible pets**, while still being based on real or imaginative cosmic concepts.

# ⭐ RARITY PROGRESSION

Give the pets a clear progression in rarity.

Use:

### 1. COMMON

The simplest cosmic concept.

* Cute and clean
* Easy-to-understand design
* Unique silhouette
* Little or no special effects
* Should still be clearly cosmic without relying on textures

### 2. UNCOMMON

More interesting and visually distinct.

* More unusual cosmic concept
* More interesting body structure
* Slightly more detail
* Very subtle animation/effect if appropriate

### 3. RARE

Clearly more impressive.

* Stronger cosmic concept
* More unique silhouette
* More interesting materials/details
* Small animated cosmic effect is okay

### 4. EPIC

A noticeably special pet.

* More complex cosmic phenomenon
* More impressive design
* Strong visual identity
* Interesting idle animation
* Some controlled particles/effects are okay

### 5. LEGENDARY

This should feel **spectacular**.

The Legendary pet should have:

* An extremely unique cosmic concept
* A memorable silhouette
* A **cosmic aura**
* 1–2 high-quality special effects
* A noticeable idle animation
* Potential effects such as:

  * Orbiting celestial objects
  * Energy trails
  * Floating cosmic particles
  * Gravitational distortion
  * Constellation effects
  * Small celestial rings
  * Cosmic energy
  * Space-time distortion

However, KEEP IT CLEAN.

Do not cover the entire pet in particles.

The effects should **enhance the design**, not hide it.

### 6. MYTHIC / SECRET

This is the ultimate Cosmic pet.

It should feel like the kind of pet players would be extremely excited to obtain.

It needs:

* An incredibly creative cosmic concept
* A unique silhouette that instantly stands out
* Premium-looking materials
* A strong **aura**
* Multiple but controlled special effects
* An impressive idle animation
* A visual effect that makes it feel genuinely rare

Possible ideas include:

* A miniature galaxy orbiting around the pet
* A black-hole-like core
* Celestial rings
* Gravitational distortion
* Shooting stars
* Nebula energy
* Space-time effects
* Constellation halos
* Floating celestial fragments
* Cosmic energy trails

Again, **do not just throw every effect onto the pet.**

The Mythic/Secret should look **powerful and premium, not messy.**

---

# 🎯 IMPORTANT: RARITY SHOULD NOT JUST MEAN MORE GLOW

Do not make the progression:

Common = normal
Rare = glowing
Legendary = more glowing
Mythic = REALLY glowing

Instead, the **actual cosmic concept should become more extraordinary** as the rarity increases.

For example:

Common → simple celestial object
Uncommon → unusual celestial object
Rare → interesting astronomical phenomenon
Epic → powerful cosmic phenomenon
Legendary → extremely rare/spectacular phenomenon
Mythic/Secret → something that feels almost impossible or universe-defining

The rarity should be communicated through **design quality, concept, silhouette, animation, and effects**, not just brightness.
